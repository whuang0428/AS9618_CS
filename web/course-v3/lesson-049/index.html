Abstraction and purposeful models

Select the details a solution needs, explain what can be omitted, and construct a model for a stated purpose.

Before you start

A route planner chooses the shortest journey. Must its model record the colour of each bus?

Check your answer

No, unless colour affects the stated route choice. Stops, connections and journey costs are relevant.

Core explanation

Abstraction removes unnecessary detail while retaining the essential features of a problem or system. What counts as essential depends on the purpose: a journey planner needs connections and travel times, whereas a vehicle-painting system may need colours.

A simpler representation reduces the information a designer must consider and makes the important relationships easier to understand. It can also reduce the data and processing needed by the solution. Omitting a detail that changes the required result makes the model unsuitable, even if the model becomes smaller.

Worked example · An indoor navigation model

  1. Task

    Find a step-free route between two rooms.

  2. Select

    Retain room entrances, corridor connections, lifts and whether a connection has steps.

  3. Discard

    Wall-paint colours do not affect the required route.

  4. Evaluate

    A model that omits steps could recommend an inaccessible route; simplicity alone is insufficient.

Common misconception

Abstraction does not mean removing all detail, and a detail is not irrelevant in every possible system.

Check your understanding

Why might stairs be essential in one route model?

Check your answer

A step-free route must distinguish stairs from accessible connections.

Core explanation

Start by stating what the model must answer. Select the objects, properties and relationships needed for that answer, and represent them with a suitable table or diagram. Record assumptions so the intended limits of the model are clear.

For a library's loan-availability check, a copy identifier and its current loan status can be enough. A title alone cannot distinguish two copies with different availability. Test the representation with one available copy and one borrowed copy before deciding that it captures the required distinction.

Construct a useful representation

  1. PurposeAnswer whether a specified copy can be borrowed now.
  2. Entities and propertiesRepresent each physical copy with a unique ID and current loan state.
  3. AssumptionReservations and repairs are outside this simplified task.
  4. Try a caseB105 is available under these rules; B104 is not.
Common misconception

A description of what to omit is not itself the requested model; show the retained information and its structure.

Check your understanding

Would storing only one status per title answer the copy-level question?

Check your answer

No. Different copies of the same title can have different loan states.

Question 1 · Command word: Explain3 marks

Explain why a fire-escape model retains exits and blocked corridors but omits classroom display colours.

S9.01.A01S9.01.A02S9.01.A03
Answer and marking points
  1. Exits and corridor accessibility determine whether an escape route can be used.
  2. Display colours do not change route availability in the stated task.
  3. Removing unrelated display detail makes the routes and obstructions easier to inspect.

Common error: Relate relevance to finding a usable escape route.

Question 3 · Command word: Explain2 marks

A bus model used to choose the lowest fare stores stops and travel times but no fares. Explain the defect and state a change.

S9.01.A01S9.01.A04
Answer and marking points
  1. Travel time does not establish which route costs least.
  2. The model needs fare information and any relevant fare rules for the available journeys.

Common error: A model must retain information needed for its particular output.

Question 1 · Command word: Explain · 3 marks

A greenhouse simulation predicts room temperature. It records room volume, heater power and insulation, but excludes the brand printed on plant pots. Explain how abstraction is used and give a benefit.

Answer and marking points
  • The model keeps physical properties that affect the predicted temperature.
  • It excludes an unrelated pot-brand detail for this prediction.
  • The reduced representation lets the designer concentrate on thermal influences.

Common error: Do not claim that every detail about plants is irrelevant to every temperature model.

Question 3 · Command word: Explain · 3 marks

A delivery planner removes all bridge-height limits from its road model. Trucks have different heights. Explain why this abstraction can produce an unsuitable route, and describe a correction.

Answer and marking points
  • A selected road may pass under a bridge too low for the truck.
  • Bridge clearance must be retained for affected road connections.
  • The model also needs the truck's height to exclude incompatible connections.

Common error: Explain the missing relationship, rather than simply requesting more detail.

Use contrasting cases and stated assumptions to detect missing distinctions.
